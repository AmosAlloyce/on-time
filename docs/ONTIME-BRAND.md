# On-Time brand system

## Positioning

On-Time makes it fast and uncomplicated to offer time, share a link, and get booked. The product combines minimalist, monochrome aesthetics with enterprise-grade reliability and security.

**Primary promise:** Clean, confident scheduling without distraction.

**Tagline:** On Time all the time.

**Voice:** Direct, useful, precise, and human. Prefer crisp verbs such as “Schedule,” “Book,” “Connect,” and “Meet.” Avoid inflated productivity jargon.

## Logo & Visual Assets

The mark communicates modern efficiency, speed, and precision.

- Use the horizontal lockup when navigation space allows.
- Use the square mark for favicons, avatars, and compact navigation.
- Keep clear space equal to one quarter of the mark width.
- Production assets:
  - `/icon.svg` is the square app mark.
  - `/ontime-logo.svg` is the horizontal lockup.

## Color Palette (Monochrome Aesthetic)

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary / Accent | Obsidian Black | `#09090b` | Buttons, headers, brand emphasis |
| Primary Hover | Carbon | `#18181b` | Hover states, active controls |
| Secondary / Borders | Zinc Border | `#e4e4e7` | Card borders, dividers, subtle outlines |
| Text (Primary) | Dark Charcoal | `#18181b` | High contrast headings and body text |
| Text (Muted) | Muted Slate | `#71717a` | Captions, secondary labels, metadata |
| Surface (Cards) | Pure White | `#ffffff` | Background for panels and interactive cards |
| Surface (Canvas) | Crisp Light | `#f4f4f5` | Page backgrounds and subtle container fills |

Status colors (green, amber, red) are reserved strictly for operational status and error feedback.

## Typography and UI

Use Inter or the native system sans-serif stack. Headlines are compact and confident with tight tracking. Body copy stays clean and legible. Surfaces use a sophisticated monochrome theme with crisp borders and clean contrast.

## Naming

Write the product name as `On-Time` with a hyphen and capitalized letters.
